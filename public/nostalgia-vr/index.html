Participant Guarantee

What you will experience in our project?

This project is designed as a short, voluntary, and reflective VR experience. It uses nostalgia to help participants think about memory, care, and social responsibility, but it does not try to force a donation or control what users should feel.

01

Before the headset

You will receive a plain-language explanation of the experience, including its approximate length, emotional theme, and charity connection. You will be reminded that participation is completely voluntary and that you can stop at any point.

02

During the VR experience

You will enter a short, guided scene inspired by common feelings of famliy reunion, primary school memory, childhood memories and belonging. The scene will avoid graphic, shocking, or fear-based content. It is designed for your own thoughts and reflections, not forcing you to make donations.

03

After the experience

You may sit quietly, write a brief reflection, ask questions, or leave. Donation and volunteering options will be presented only after the VR portion is over, so the choice remains separate and voluntary to ensure the lowest level of manipulation.

04

No manipulation

The project will not use hidden persuasion, guilt-based scripts, countdown pressure, personalized emotional targeting, or deceptive statistics. The website and staff will make it clear that saying “no” is an acceptable outcome.

05

Privacy

This prototype form is for demonstration. In a real version, only necessary reservation information would be collected. Reflection responses would be optional, and personal data would not be sold or shared for unrelated advertising.

06

Accessibility and care

Participants who are uncomfortable with VR, motion, or emotional themes may choose an alternative text or video version. The experience should support empathy without excluding people who cannot or do not want to use a headset.
